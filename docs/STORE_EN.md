# YKS Preparation — Store Copy

## Short description

Track YKS topics, mock-exam net scores, and goals fully offline.

## Description

YKS Preparation helps students browse the TYT and AYT topic taxonomy, mark topic progress, save section-by-section mock-exam results, and understand net-score trends. Students can browse official YÖK Atlas undergraduate programs, while the calendar and news screens link to official announcements. The app does not produce synthetic score or rank estimates when no verified personal model exists.

- No account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.
- User data stays on the device.
- The core app works offline from first launch.
- Data can be exported and restored as a JSON backup.
- Turkish and English, with light and dark themes.

This is not an official ÖSYM or YÖK product and does not reproduce ÖSYM question text. Score/rank estimation is unavailable, and unpublished values are not filled with synthetic data. Admissions decisions must not rely solely on this app.
